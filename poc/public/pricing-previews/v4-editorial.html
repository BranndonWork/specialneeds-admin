V4: Editorial / Magazine — Typography IS the design. Playfair Display (headings) + Space Mono (prices/labels) + DM Sans (body). Prices are typographic pull quotes — unapologetically large. Strict B&W palette with one red accent. Horizontal comparison grid (magazine feature table). Grid-breaking column headers. Zero gradients, zero soft edges. Inspired by Monocle, The Atlantic, NYT product pages. Authority through restraint.
